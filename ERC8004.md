### Honest status (v1)
v1 validation is **self-attested**: the agent owner requests validation and the relay (same ops set) responds after re-reading the on-chain/stream content hash. This is a standard-shaped Validation artifact for indexers — it is **NOT independent third-party validation**. An outside validator can call `validationResponse` later with zero contract changes. Do not market as "independently validated."

### Contract constraint note
The reference ValidationRegistry rejects `validatorAddress == msg.sender`. So when the relay is operator, it cannot also be the validator in the same `validationRequest` call. v1 sample therefore uses: **agent owner → validationRequest(validator=relay)**, then **relay → validationResponse**.
